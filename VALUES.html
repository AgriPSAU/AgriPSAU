Core Values

Pampanga State Agricultural University is guided by strong principles that promote responsible leadership, meaningful service, innovation, and sustainable development for the community and nation.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

People-Centered

PSAU values the welfare, growth, and empowerment of students, employees, stakeholders, and communities.

🧠

Systems Thinking

The university promotes critical thinking, innovation, and understanding of interconnected systems for effective problem-solving.

✅

Accountability

PSAU encourages responsibility, transparency, integrity, and commitment in all academic and administrative actions.

🤝

Unity

The institution fosters collaboration, teamwork, respect, and harmony among all members of the university.

Commitment to Excellence

Guided by these core values, Pampanga State Agricultural University continues to provide relevant education, social responsibility, sustainable development, and innovative services that contribute to national and global progress.
