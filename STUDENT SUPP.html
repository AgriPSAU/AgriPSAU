Student Support Services Office

📘 Brief History

The Student Support Services Office is part of the university. It helps students with their needs, concerns, and overall development.

🎯 Mission

To support students by providing helpful services that improve their learning, well-being, and personal growth.

🌍 Vision

The office aims to create a safe, supportive, and student-friendly environment that helps students succeed in school and life.

🎓 Goals & Objectives

1. Student Services
  • Help students with concerns and needs
  • Provide guidance and assistance
  • Support student well-being
2. Academic Support
  • Assist students in their studies
  • Provide learning improvement programs
  • Guide academic concerns
3. Personal Development
  • Promote good values and behavior
  • Build confidence and skills
  • Encourage student participation
4. Programs & Activities
  • Organize seminars and workshops
  • Conduct student activities
  • Promote leadership and teamwork
5. Coordination & Support
  • Work with other offices
  • Provide clear information
  • Improve services based on feedback

⚠️ Proper Conduct in the Office

  • Be respectful and polite
  • Follow office rules
  • Wait for your turn
  • Speak clearly about concerns
