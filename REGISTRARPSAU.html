Registrar’s Office

📄 Brief History

The Registrar’s Office is located inside the university and serves students, faculty, and staff. It handles student records and important school documents.

🎯 Mission

To provide accurate records, fast service, and updated systems that support students, teachers, and staff.

🌍 Vision

The Registrar’s Office aims to be modern, organized, and trusted in managing student records and services, and to be at par with other institutions.

🎓 Goals & Objectives

1. Services to Students
  • Provide correct and updated records
  • Listen to student feedback
  • Improve services based on student needs
2. Services to Faculty & Staff
  • Support academic and administrative needs
  • Work together for better record management
  • Assist in school processes
3. Records Management
  • Keep records safe and organized
  • Update and check data regularly
  • Provide easy access to documents
4. Technology & Systems
  • Use digital systems for faster service
  • Improve online services
  • Upgrade systems when needed
5. Staff Development
  • Add staff if needed
  • Attend trainings and seminars
6. Promotion & Awareness
  • Inform students about registrar services
  • Guide students on requirements
  • Coordinate with other offices

⚠️ Proper Conduct in the Office

  • Be respectful and patient
  • Follow instructions and procedures
  • Keep the area quiet and orderly
  • No cutting of lines
  • Prepare complete requirements before transactions
