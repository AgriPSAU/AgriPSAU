History of Pampanga State Agricultural University

History of PSAU

Discover the inspiring journey of Pampanga State Agricultural University from its humble beginnings to becoming one of the leading agricultural institutions in the Philippines.

🌱

1885

PSAU started as Estacion Pecuaria, a livestock station established during the Spanish period in Magalang, Pampanga.

🏫

Farm School Era

The institution later evolved into an agricultural farm school, helping students gain knowledge and skills in farming and livestock.

📚

1965

It officially became Pampanga Agricultural College (PAC), expanding its academic programs and research initiatives.

🔬

Research & Technology

PAC became known for innovation, agricultural research, and technology development benefiting communities and farmers.

🎓

2013

Through Republic Act 10605, Pampanga Agricultural College officially became Pampanga State Agricultural University (PSAU).

🌍

Today

PSAU is now recognized as a premier institution focusing on agriculture, technology, education, innovation, and global competitiveness.
