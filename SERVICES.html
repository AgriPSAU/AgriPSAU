Health Services Office

🏥 Brief History

The Health Services Office is located inside the university and provides basic medical care and health support to students, faculty, and staff.

🎯 Mission

To give quality health care, promote wellness, and provide fast and reliable medical services to the university community.

🌍 Vision

The Health Services Office aims to be modern, trusted, and effective in promoting good health and safety for everyone in the university.

🎓 Goals & Objectives

1. Services to Students
  • Provide basic medical care and first aid
  • Monitor student health
  • Give health advice and support
2. Services to Faculty & Staff
  • Offer medical assistance
  • Support workplace health and safety
  • Assist during emergencies
3. Health Programs & Services
  • Conduct health check-ups
  • Promote vaccination and wellness programs
  • Teach proper hygiene and healthy lifestyle
4. Facilities & Equipment
  • Keep clinic clean and safe
  • Ensure medicines and tools are available
  • Improve equipment when needed
5. Staff Development
  • Train health staff regularly
  • Attend seminars and workshops
6. Promotion & Awareness
  • Conduct health seminars and campaigns
  • Encourage healthy habits
  • Work with other offices for programs

⚠️ Proper Conduct in the Clinic

  • Be respectful and follow instructions
  • Keep quiet and maintain cleanliness
  • Wait for your turn
  • Give correct health information
  • Follow clinic rules
